## What this is

Part of this repository was assembled from research dumps produced by AI search
tools. Those dumps arrive with two kinds of links mixed together: citations the
compilation actually used for a specific claim, and raw search hits that were
returned but never attached to anything.

Everything in the first category has been placed in the theme file where its
claim lives. Everything in the second category is here.

**These are not citations for anything in this repository.** No claim rests on
them. They are retained under the standing rule that no reference is dropped, and
because a portion may prove worth pursuing.

## What this is not

A bibliography. Several entries below are state media, aggregator reprints,
opinion columns, party organs, social posts, or library catalog records for
books about Trump. Citing them as sourcing would undermine the rest of the work.
They function as leads to underlying reporting, not as references.

Entries are grouped by category to prevent one kind being mistaken for
another.

## Why these are retained rather than deleted

Two reasons.

The first is procedural: the standing rule in this repository is that no
reference is dropped, and an unrecorded exception defeats the rule.

The second is substantive. A research dump of 150 links resembles 150 citations
and is not. Roughly a third of the material that arrived was state media, catalog
records, social posts, and opinion columns. Folded into the main files as though
it were sourcing, it would have produced a document that read as thoroughly
referenced while being substantially padded. Segregating it is the necessary
work; keeping the discarded material visible allows the segregation to be
audited.
