# War Powers and Military Strikes

Lethal force abroad, ordered without congressional authorization — from the 2020
Soleimani strike through the boat campaign, the seizure of Venezuela, and the
2026 Iran war.

## The precedent: Soleimani, January 2020

The first term's test case, and the template for everything below: kill a
foreign state official, assert Article II, tell Congress afterward.

- **Department of Defense statement, January 2, 2020:** "At the direction of the
  President, the U.S. military has taken decisive defensive action to protect
  U.S. personnel abroad by killing Qasem Soleimani, the head of the Islamic
  Revolutionary Guard Corps-Quds Force, a U.S.-designated Foreign Terrorist
  Organization."
  ([DoD release](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/2049534/statement-by-the-department-of-defense/#:~:text=At%20the%20direction%20of%20the%20President,%20the%20U.S.%20military%20has%20taken%20decisive%20defensive%20action%20to%20protect%20U.S.%20personnel%20abroad%20by%20killing%20Qasem%20Soleimani,%20the%20head%20of%20the%20Islamic%20Revolutionary%20Guard%20Corps-Quds%20Force,%20a%20U.S.-designated%20Foreign%20Terrorist%20Organization))

- **Trump's remarks, January 3, 2020:** "Last night, at my direction, the United
  States military successfully executed a flawless precision strike that killed
  the number-one terrorist anywhere in the world."
  ([White House archive](https://trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov/briefings-statements/remarks-president-trump-killing-qasem-soleimani/#:~:text=at%20my%20direction,%20the%20United%20States%20military%20successfully%20executed%20a%20flawless%20precision%20strike%20that%20killed%20the%20number-one%20terrorist%20anywhere%20in%20the%20world))

- **The OLC memo**, released under FOIA, is the document at the center of the
  legality fight. It justified the strike under Article II plus the 2002 Iraq
  AUMF, concluding that "the President could reasonably determine that the
  nature, scope, and duration of hostilities directly resulting from the strike
  against Soleimani would not rise to the level of a war."
  ([DOJ, PDF](https://www.justice.gov/d9/2023-04/2020-03-10_soleimani_airstrike_redacted_2021.pdf#:~:text=we%20concluded%20that%20the%20President%20could%20reasonably%20determine%20that%20the%20nature,scope,%20and%20duration%20of%20hostilities%20directly%20resulting%20from%20the%20strike%20against%20Soleimani%20would%20not%20rise%20to%20the%20level%20of%20a%20war))

  The operative legal theory is that a killing does not constitute war so long as
  the executive predicts it will not lead to one. That reasoning underpins every
  section below.

- **Congress's response:** Senator Kaine's S.J.Res. 68 war powers resolution
  (2020), which passed both chambers and was vetoed.

The contemporaneous entry, including the shifting "imminent threat" claim and
the questions about its timing, is in
[Russia and Foreign Policy](russia-and-foreign-policy.md#10-the-soleimani-strike)
and [Timelines and Open Questions](timelines-and-open-questions.md).

## 1. The boat strikes

Since September 2, 2025, the United States has conducted at least **64 lethal
strikes on civilian boats** in the Caribbean and eastern Pacific, killing at
least **210 people**. Trump and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth publicly posted
video of the strikes.
([ACLU](https://www.aclu.org/press-releases/court-to-hear-arguments-in-lawsuit-demanding-trump-admin-publicly-release-legal-rationale-for-illegal-boat-strikes#:~:text=Since%20Sept.%202,%202025,%20the%20Trump%20administration%20has%20conducted%20at%20least%2064%20strikes%20on%20civilian%20boats%20in%20the%20Caribbean,%20murdering%20at%20least%20210%20civilians))

**The first lawsuit.** *Burnley v. United States*, filed in the District of
Massachusetts on January 27, 2026, by the families of **Chad Joseph** and
**Rishi Samaroo** — two Trinidadian men killed on October 14, 2025 while
returning from fishing and farm work. Trinidad's foreign minister confirmed they
had no drug connections. The claims are wrongful death under the Death on the
High Seas Act and extrajudicial killing under the Alien Tort Statute.
([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/01/27/g-s1-107510/drug-boat-strikes-lawsuit-families#:~:text=Relatives%20of%20two%20Trinidadian%20men%20killed%20in%20an%20airstrike%20last%20October%20are%20suing%20the%20U.S.%20government%20for%20wrongful%20death%20and%20for%20carrying%20out%20extrajudicial%20killings))

**The secret memo.** The ACLU, the Center for Constitutional Rights, and the
NYCLU filed a FOIA suit seeking the Office of Legal Counsel opinion behind the
strikes — an opinion that reportedly does not merely authorize them but purports
to **immunize from future criminal prosecution the personnel who authorized or
carried them out**. Oral argument was held June 24, 2026.
([ACLU](https://www.aclu.org/press-releases/court-to-hear-arguments-in-lawsuit-demanding-trump-admin-publicly-release-legal-rationale-for-illegal-boat-strikes#:~:text=seeking%20immediate%20disclosure%20of%20the%20OLC%20opinion%20that%20not%20only%20provides%20the%20legal%20reasoning%20and%20governing%20parameters%20for%20the%20strikes%20but%20reportedly%20purports%20to%20immunize%20personnel%20who%20authorized%20or%20took%20part%20in%20these%20unlawful%20strikes%20from%20future%20criminal%20prosecution))

A government that secures itself a secret prosecution immunity before the
killing begins has documented its own assessment of the killing's legality.

**The evidence is self-published.** Trump's own posts — including video of the
October 14 "lethal kinetic strike" — are the primary record. There is no dispute
about whether these happened. The dispute is only about whether anything can be
done about it.

## 2. Venezuela

### The capture of Maduro

**Real.** "Operation Absolute Resolve," January 3, 2026: U.S. forces seized
Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro and his wife Cilia Flores in a predawn raid
on his compound in Caracas. Roughly 150 aircraft launched simultaneously from 20
locations; no U.S. personnel were killed.
([UK House of Commons Library briefing](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10452/#:~:text=On%203%20January%202026,%20the%20United%20States%20captured%20Venezuelan%20President%20Nicol%C3%A1s%20Maduro%20and%20his%20wife,%20Cilia%20Flores,%20in%20a%20military%20operation%20in%20Caracas),
[Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/01/venezuela-150-aircraft-cyber-effects-maduro-operation-how-it-happened-caine/),
[Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/us-military-details-timeline-operation-capture-maduro-revealing-more-than-150-aircraft-involved))

**The legal basis claimed.** Attorney General Pam Bondi stated that Maduro had
already been indicted in the Southern District of New York on narco-terrorism
conspiracy, cocaine importation conspiracy, and weapons charges; the unsealed
indictment is the primary document.
([House of Commons Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10452/#:~:text=been%20indicted%20in%20the%20Southern%20District%20of%20New%20York%E2%80%9D%20and%20that%20Mr%20Maduro%20had%20been%20charged%20with%20%E2%80%9CNarco-Terrorism%20Conspiracy,%20Cocaine%20Importation%20Conspiracy,%20Possession%20of%20Machineguns%20and%20Destructive%20Devices))

**The dispute.** Whether a military raid into a sovereign capital to seize a
sitting head of state is a lawful arrest on an indictment, or a kidnapping with
paperwork, is contested among scholars. "Kidnapping" is the critics'
characterization, not a finding.
([Army War College analysis](https://ssi.armywarcollege.edu/SSI-Media/Recent-Publications/Article/4370829/how-maduros-capture-went-down-a-military-strategist-explains-what-goes-into-a-s/),
[The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/how-maduros-capture-went-down-a-military-strategist-explains-what-goes-into-a-successful-special-op-272671),
[House Republican Policy Committee memo, PDF](https://republicanpolicy.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/republicanpolicy.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/rpc-operation-absolute-resove-memo.pdf))

### The oil

No interpretation is required here. Trump, at the January 3, 2026 press
conference:

> "We're going to have our very large United States oil companies, the biggest
> anywhere in the world, go in, spend billions of dollars, fix the badly broken
> infrastructure, the oil infrastructure, and start making money for the
> country."

([House of Commons Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10452/#:~:text=We%E2%80%99re%20going%20to%20have%20our%20very%20large%20United%20States%20oil%20companies,%20the%20biggest%20anywhere%20in%20the%20world,%20go%20in,%20spend%20billions%20of%20dollars,%20fix%20the%20badly%20broken%20infrastructure,%20the%20oil%20infrastructure,%20and%20start%20making%20money%20for%20the%20country);
the C-SPAN and White House transcripts are the underlying primary record.)

### The government

> "We are going to run the country, until we can do a safe, proper and judicious
> transition."
> ([House of Commons Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10452/#:~:text=We%20are%20going%20to%20run%20the%20country,%20until%20we%20can%20do%20a%20safe,%20proper%20and%20judicious%20transition))

Vice President Delcy Rodríguez was sworn in as interim president on January 5,
2026 — formally before Venezuela's own National Assembly, but under U.S.
military occupation, which is why critics call the government installed rather
than succeeded. Venezuela's police and military pledged loyalty to her later
that month.
([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nicolas-maduro-replaced-venezuela-president-delcy-rodriguez-who-is-she/),
[Al Jazeera on the swearing-in](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/1/5/delcy-rodriguez-sworn-in-as-venezuelas-interim-president),
[Al Jazeera on the loyalty pledges](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/1/29/venezuelas-police-military-pledge-loyalty-to-interim-president-rodriguez))

"Fake government" is a characterization, not an official finding. The documented
facts — a head of state seized by foreign troops, a successor sworn in two days
later under occupation, and the occupying president announcing his oil
companies are going in — carry the argument without it.

## 3. War with Iran, without authorization

### Phase one: June 2025

U.S. strikes on Iranian nuclear sites. No congressional authorization was
sought. A war powers resolution was introduced June 23, 2025 by Representatives
Meeks, Smith, and Himes:

> "President Trump must not be allowed to start a war with Iran … without
> Congressional approval."
> ([Rep. Adam Smith](http://adamsmith.house.gov/news/press-releases/smith-meeks-himes-introduce-war-powers-resolution-cease-us-hostilities-iran#:~:text=President%20Trump%20must%20not%20be%20allowed%20to%20start%20a%20war%20with%20Iran%20without%20Congressional%20approval))

Massie and Khanna's H.Con.Res. 38 failed in the House that month.

### Phase two: open war, February 28, 2026

**The assassination of Ali Khamenei.** On February 28, 2026, Iran's supreme
leader was killed in Tehran during a joint U.S.–Israeli operation, with CIA
intelligence used to locate senior Iranian officials. Iran confirmed his death
on March 1. Satellite imagery showed his residence severely damaged. His
daughter, son-in-law, and grandson were also killed.
([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/daughter-grandchild-irans-khamenei-killed-us-israeli-strikes-state-media-says-2026-03-01/),
[NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/02/28/nx-s1-5730158/israel-iran-strikes-trump-us),
[The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/02/28/israel-strikes-iran-live-updates/),
[PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-iranian-supreme-leader-ali-khamenei-killed-in-u-s-israeli-strikes),
[CNN live coverage](https://www.cnn.com/world/live-news/israel-iran-attack-02-28-26-hnk-intl),
[Al Jazeera, day two](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/1/us-israel-attacks-on-iran-day-2-khamenei-is-killed-iran-retaliates),
[overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Ali_Khamenei),
[the war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war))

**Note on sourcing:** A claim of this magnitude requires major-outlet
corroboration, which the Reuters, NPR, Washington Post, and PBS citations above
supply. One frequently circulated citation for the assassination —
[islamicinfocenter.com](https://islamicinfocenter.com/ayatollah-khamenei-death/) —
is recorded here for completeness and is not adequate sourcing on its own.

**The authorization that never came.** Trump's March 2, 2026 war powers report
to Congress cited only his Article II authority. As Lawfare put it, the report
"does not address why the military carried out the attack without prior
congressional authorization" — because there was no answer to give.
([Lawfare](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/white-house-submits-iran-war-powers-report-to-congress#:~:text=does%20not%20address%20why%20the%20military%20carried%20out%20the%20attack%20without%20prior%20congressional%20authorization))

The administration later argued, in a May 1, 2026 letter, that a ceasefire had
"stopped the 60-day clock" under the War Powers Resolution.

**Congress finally acted — and it did not matter.**

- Senate discharged S.J.Res. 185, 50–47, May 19, 2026
- House passed H.Con.Res. 86, 215–208, June 3, 2026
- The Senate then passed a war powers measure in June 2026 — **the first time in
  the history of the War Powers Resolution that Congress passed one**
  ([Lawfare](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/what-congressional-resolutions-mean-for-the-war-in-iran),
  [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ce8j6g3v3r4o))

- Strikes resumed July 7, 2026. Trump notified Congress in a two-page letter on
  July 10, "in line with the War Powers Act."
  ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/defense/5966415-trump-congress-resumes-strikes-iran/#:~:text=Trump%20told%20lawmakers%20in%20a%202-page%20letter%20on%20Friday%20that%20U.S.%20strikes%20resumed%20against%20Iran%20on%20July%207,%20alerting%20legislators%20in%20line%20with%20the%20War%20Powers%20Act))

Congress crossed a threshold it had never crossed in the fifty-year history of
the War Powers Resolution, and the war continued regardless.

## 4. The Minab school strike

**February 28, 2026** — the first day of the air campaign. Three missiles struck
the **Shajareh Tayyebeh girls' elementary school** in Minab, Hormozgan Province,
in rapid succession, during class time. Iranian authorities put the final toll
at roughly 165 dead — most of them girls aged 7 to 12. Iranian state media
reported 168–180.
([2026 Minab school attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Minab_school_attack),
[TIME](https://time.com/7381982/iran-girls-school-killed/),
[Al Jazeera investigation](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/3/questions-over-minab-girls-school-strike-as-israel-us-deny-involvement),
[Simple English summary](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Minab_school_attack),
[AIR News](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/iran-57-students-killed-in-strike-on-a-girls-school-in-minab-city))

**How it happened.** The Washington Post reported that the school was on a U.S.
target list and may have been mistaken for a military site. NPR's satellite
imagery showed the school walled off from the adjacent IRGC naval base. CNN
reported that commanders bypassed warnings about outdated targeting data.
Reuters reported that U.S. investigators themselves believed American forces
were likely responsible.
([The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/03/11/us-strike-iran-elementary-school-ai-target-list/))

The mechanism, per the preliminary U.S. military inquiry: the Defense
Intelligence Agency had the building classified as a legitimate military target,
a classification dating from when the site was part of the adjacent IRGC naval
base. The intelligence was stale. Nobody checked.

Independent investigations by the New York Times, NPR, and CBC reached the same
conclusion about responsibility.

**Accountability.** Hegseth announced a Pentagon investigation on March 13,
2026. Its findings have not been released.
([Truthout, "The Investigation Into the Minab Elementary School Bombing Is Done. Where Is It?"](https://truthout.org/articles/the-investigation-into-the-minab-elementary-school-bombing-is-done-where-is-it/))

**The findings that have been released:**

- Amnesty International: those responsible for the "deadly and unlawful US
  strike on school that killed over 100 children must be held accountable."
  ([Amnesty](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/03/usa-iran-those-responsible-for-deadly-and-unlawful-us-strike-on-school-that-killed-over-100-children-must-be-held-accountable/))
- Human Rights Watch's legal analysis of whether it was a war crime.
  ([HRW](https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/04/20/was-the-attack-on-an-iranian-primary-school-a-war-crime))
- Just Security's targeting-law analysis.
  ([Just Security](https://www.justsecurity.org/134350/legal-analysis-minab-school-strike/))
- Senator Van Hollen's letter to Hegseth demanding the civilian casualty
  record.
  ([PDF](https://www.vanhollen.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/letter_to_hegseth_on_minab_bombing_civcas_iran.pdf))
- **September 2026:** a UN-commissioned fact-finding mission found "reasonable
  grounds" to believe the United States committed war crimes, citing the two
  February 28 strikes.

One child, **Makan Nasiri**, was still listed as missing two months later.
([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/23/makan-nasiri-the-only-missing-child-from-the-school-bombed-in-irans-minab))

## 5. Civilian infrastructure in Iran

From March through July 2026, U.S. strikes hit bridges, power facilities,
tunnels, and ports across southern Iran. Iranian Red Crescent estimates:
**~1,900 killed, 20,000 injured, 115,000+ civilian facilities damaged.**
([CGTN](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-04-03/Iran-war-enters-dangerous-new-phase-as-US-strikes-civilian-facilities-1M30fbpdD7G/p.html),
[AP](https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-hormuz-strait-war-july-22-2026-42ff3de8d135ad72ff3ba4d94cc0921d),
[The Star](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/world/2026-07-17-us-launches-new-strikes-as-iran-says-civilian-infrastructure-hit))

**The primary source is the president's own account of the policy.** On Truth
Social, July 2026:

> "any time the Islamic Republic of Iran shoots at a ship in the Strait of
> Hormuz … the United States will bomb and destroy ONE BRIDGE OR POWER PLANT"

And earlier, threatening "completely obliterating all of their Electric
Generating Plants, Oil Wells and Kharg Island (and possibly all desalinization
plants!)."

This is a stated policy of destroying civilian infrastructure on a one-for-one
retaliation schedule, announced in advance, in writing, by the person ordering
it. There is no interpretive step between the evidence and the charge.

**UN High Commissioner for Human Rights Volker Türk:** "deliberately attacking
civilians and civilian infrastructure is a war crime."

**The administration's defense** is that IRGC use of these facilities makes them
lawful dual-use targets. Legal scholars dispute both the factual predicate and
the proportionality analysis. That is the live legal fight, and it is worth
stating fairly rather than assuming away.
