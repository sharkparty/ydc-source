## Cold open

**#77** "It's going to disappear. One day, it's like a miracle, it will
disappear." It didn't. More than a million Americans were
==dead==^[✏️ caption: "gone"] by May 2022. And you didn't care.

## The run

**#75** You didn't care that his administration spent two years cutting the
programs built to catch a pandemic, and there's video of him saying the staff
could be "rehired when needed." They were needed.

**#76** You didn't care that in July 2019, months before the outbreak, his
administration cut the CDC epidemiologist stationed in China, whose whole job
was to warn us early. Then he blamed China for not warning us early.

**#78** You didn't care when he said COVID would kill "a much smaller range" than
the flu. The next day the World Health Organization put its
==death==^[✏️ caption: "d3ath"] rate among confirmed cases at three point four
percent. The flu's is about zero point one.

**#79** You didn't care that the first CDC tests were broken, a working WHO test
existed and wasn't used, and he blamed an Obama rule that did nothing of the
sort.

**#80** You didn't care when he said "anybody that wants a test can get a test,"
while you were standing in the line he said didn't exist.

**#81** You didn't care that he put Mike Pence in charge, the man who let an HIV
outbreak grow because he needed thirty days to pray over a needle exchange.

**#82** You didn't care that Jared Kushner, who told him the virus was fake news,
crowdsourced pandemic policy from a Facebook group.

**#84** You didn't care that he wouldn't order ventilators because the Chamber
of Commerce didn't want the "red tape."

## Closer

**#83** And you didn't care that he held off on declaring an emergency while he
waited to hear what Jared thought, and in the middle of it, autographed a
printout of the Dow. People were ==dying==^[✏️ caption: "d!ying"], and he was
signing a chart of the stock market.

>

> **Tired is not the same as defeated.**
> 
> You counted on outrage lasting 24 hours, on the next scandal burying the last, on everyone moving on. You counted wrong.
