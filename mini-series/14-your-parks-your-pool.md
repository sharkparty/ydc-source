## Cold open

**#126** "I have a guy who's unbelievable at doing swimming pools." That's how he
announced he was redoing the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool. No-bid contract,
to a Mar-a-Lago neighbor. The water turned green the day after the refill. The
coating peeled. Then a former Olympian got arrested for touching a loose piece.
And you didn't care.

## The run

**#146** You didn't care that the Park Service sent three hundred twenty-three
million dollars to White House grounds projects, up from seven and a half
million, while cutting Yellowstone, Yosemite and the Grand Canyon. He cut the
Grand Canyon to pay for his lawn.

**#147** You didn't care about the four-hundred-million-dollar ballroom that
Congress funded less than one percent of, until a court ordered it stopped.

**#148** You didn't care that Interior told park staff never to be the first to
confirm a death. They used to go by "maximum disclosure, minimum delay." Now they
won't tell you who died on the trail. Ask yourself who that policy protects.

**#149** You didn't care that he refused disaster aid to four blue states after
his own agencies said they qualified. Pray your hurricane hits a red state.

**#150** You didn't care that his budget went after the observatory that has
kept the planet's CO₂ record continuously since 1958, under a document titled "Ending
the Green New Scam." Don't want to debate climate science? Pull up your
homeowner's insurance bill and tell me who believes it's real.

## Closer

**#175** And you didn't care when a federal judge ruled it illegal to put his
name on the Kennedy Center, his board put it back up anyway, and then the
Kennedy Center closed. He'd rather close a national institution than take his
name off it.
