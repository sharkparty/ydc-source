## Cold open

**#17** He used charity money to buy a portrait of himself. A court called his
foundation "a shocking pattern of illegality" and shut it down. You didn't
care.

## The run

**#15** You didn't care that he ran a fake university. Up to thirty-five
thousand dollars a head for "secrets" that never showed up. He settled for
twenty-five million, with more than six thousand victims, right before his
inauguration.

**#16** You didn't care that Florida's attorney general sat on the Trump
University complaints, took twenty-five grand from his foundation four days
after saying she might join the case, and then didn't. He made her Attorney
General of the United States.

**#21** You didn't care that he flailed his arms on stage to mock a reporter's
disability, and the crowd laughed like it was the best night of their lives.

**#22** You didn't care when he said John McCain wasn't a war hero. "I like
people who weren't captured." Five draft deferments. Bone spurs. A doctor's
note.

**#23** You didn't care when he spent days attacking a Gold Star family, the
parents of an Army captain killed in Iraq. No regrets. The magnet on your truck
has done more for them than he ever has.

## Closer

**#18** And you didn't care that after 9/11 he promised ten thousand dollars to
the Twin Towers Fund, and nobody has ever found proof he paid a dime. You wear
"Never Forget." He forgot the check.

## Sign-off


> Congratulations to everyone who survived another harrowing episode of **“How The Fuck Is This Still Happening?”**
> 
> It’s been a lot. A genuinely fucking unbelievable amount of a lot. If you’re tired, you’re not fucking imagining it.
> 
> To the people who made all this happen: **We noticed.** The lies. The cruelty. The corruption. The hypocrisy. Every chance to do right you looked directly in the eye and decided to ignore.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal; to let exhaustion become acceptance; to let outrage become memory loss.
> 
> **Tired is not the same as defeated.**
> 
> You counted on outrage lasting 24 hours, on the next scandal burying the last, on everyone moving on. You counted wrong.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal. I refuse to stop being angry simply because there’s too much to be angry about.
> 
> As Angela Davis put it:
> “I am no longer accepting the things I cannot change.  
> I am changing the things I cannot accept.”
> 
> **So we’re saving the receipts.** Every single fucking one. Because shame doesn’t seem to be working. And that’s okay. 
> **Accountability doesn’t require your cooperation.**
> The receipt's getting longer every day. See you next time.
