## Cold open

**#69** Tariffs are paid by American importers and passed on to you. "China is
paying us billions" was never true. He put a tax on your groceries, told you
China paid it, and you believed him at the checkout. You didn't care.

## The run

**#68** *(2017)* You didn't care that he pulled out of the Trans-Pacific Partnership,
the trade deal America wrote to box China out. The other eleven countries
finished it without us. "Tough on China" meant handing China the empty chair.

**#70** *(2017–2021)* You didn't care that he promised to wipe out the national
debt, and it grew about eight trillion dollars in four years instead.

**#71** *(2017)* You didn't care that his tax cut knocked an estimated one
trillion dollars off American home values. It took money out of your house, and
you put his sign in the front yard.

**#72** *(2017–2020)* You didn't care that the "investment boom" was a
stock-buyback boom. They didn't raise your wages. They bought their own stock.

**#73** *(2020)* You didn't care that he promised not to touch Medicare,
Medicaid or Social Security, then sent Congress a budget that cut them.

**#74** *(2017–2019)* You didn't care that he took credit for "the lowest
unemployment ever" by riding the trend Obama started. Obama brought it down six
points. He added four-tenths, more slowly. He inherited a ball rolling downhill
and bragged that he'd kicked it.

**#123** *(April 2025)* You didn't care that the morning his tariffs were tanking
your 401(k), he posted "THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!!" Hours later, the pause.
The S&P jumped nine and a half percent. You held the bag. Somebody bought the
dip.

**#124** *(2025)* You didn't care that he made fifteen trades in Nvidia stock and
reported more than a thousand transactions while in office, including companies
with business before his own administration. They didn't hide it. They filed it
on a government form.

**#132** *(July 4, 2025)* You didn't care that he paid for permanent corporate
tax cuts by cutting Medicaid, the ACA and food stamps. Your mom's nursing-home
coverage paid for a billionaire's tax break, and you set off fireworks.

## Closer

**#165** *(February 2026)* And you didn't care when the Supreme Court struck his
tariffs down and left about a hundred and seventy-five billion dollars in
refunds, and the Commerce Secretary's own sons had bought up the rights to those
refunds. You paid the tariffs. His Commerce Secretary's family collected the
refunds.
