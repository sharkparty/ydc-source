## Cold open

**#125** A man stole the tax money withheld from his own employees' paychecks.
Three weeks after his mom went to a million-dollar-a-plate dinner at Mar-a-Lago,
he was pardoned, and more than four million dollars in restitution went away. And
you didn't care.

## The run

**#121** You didn't care that he pardoned a convicted fraudster who'd given nearly
two million dollars to his campaign, and whose lawyer was the Attorney General's
brother.

**#122** You didn't care that he wiped out a hundred and thirty million dollars
in fines for a crypto exchange with one signature. You'd lose your house over a
missed tax payment.

**#142** You didn't care that he pardoned the founder of Binance after Binance
built the tech behind his family's stablecoin, and then said he had "no idea
who" the guy was.

**#143** You didn't care that he let two men convicted of defrauding the Oglala
Sioux Tribe walk away owing nothing. Both had testified against Hunter Biden.
The victims were a tribal nation's pension fund.

**#144** You didn't care that he pardoned a media founder three months after a
judge gave him ten years for defrauding investors, partly by impersonating real
executives on investor calls. Or that another set of pardons wiped out nearly
fifty million dollars in restitution. Fraud's only a crime when it happens to
him.

**#182** You didn't care that his pardons erased about one point seven billion
dollars owed to victims, and that Reuters found ninety-six percent of his
second-term pardons failed the Justice Department's own guidelines. Pardon
brokers charge up to two million dollars. Justice has a price list now.

**#164** You didn't care that at least ninety-seven pardoned January 6 rioters
have been tied to new crimes since. "Tough on crime," unless the criminal wore
the hat.

**#163** You didn't care that at least fourteen of them have been charged with
sex crimes or ==child sexual abuse material==^[🔇 BLEEP · caption: "CSAM"]
offenses. You spent years calling everyone else ==groomers==^[✏️ caption:
"gr**mers"].

**#162** You didn't care that another January 6 defendant was charged with
possessing and producing ==child sexual abuse material==^[🔇 BLEEP · caption:
"CSAM"] that the FBI found *while investigating January 6*. The pardon never
read the file.

## Closer

**#161** And you didn't care that one of those pardoned men was convicted of
==molesting==^[🔇 BLEEP · caption: "abusing"] a child under twelve and sentenced
to life. While he waited for trial, he tried to buy his victim's silence with
money he said he'd be getting as January 6 restitution. He tried to pay off a
child with your guy's fund.
