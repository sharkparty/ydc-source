# E05: Running It Like a Family Business

> [!info] Episode card

## Cold open

**#37** His daughter used a personal email account for hundreds of emails about
government business. You built an entire chant around that exact thing. And you
didn't care.

## The run

**#31** You didn't care that an ethics watchdog counted more than nine hundred
conflicts of interest in his second year alone. If you had business with the
government, you just booked a room at his hotel.

**#32** You didn't care that foreign governments, China included, paid his
businesses at least seven point eight million dollars while he was president.
The Constitution has a clause written to stop exactly that.

**#33** You didn't care that China fast-tracked his company's trademarks,
including some it had refused for years. "Tough on China." China was a business
partner.

**#34** You didn't care that his first four trips to Mar-a-Lago cost you thirteen
point six million dollars, with some of it paid straight to his own club. You
screamed about Obama's vacations for eight years.

**#38** You didn't care that he fought from coast to coast to hide the tax
returns every modern president has shown. You need three pay stubs to rent a
one-bedroom.

**#39** You didn't care that he tweeted a photo from a classified intelligence
briefing to score a point about Iran. You'd court-martial a private for posting
his barracks.

## Closer

**#35** And you didn't care that he overrode his own security officials to get
top-secret clearances for his daughter and son-in-law, then denied doing it.
"Lock her up," you said, about classified information. He handed the secrets to
Jared because Jared married in.

## Sign-off


> Congratulations to everyone who survived another harrowing episode of **“How The Fuck Is This Still Happening?”**
> 
> It’s been a lot. A genuinely fucking unbelievable amount of a lot. If you’re tired, you’re not fucking imagining it.
> 
> To the people who made all this happen: **We noticed.** The lies. The cruelty. The corruption. The hypocrisy. Every chance to do right you looked directly in the eye and decided to ignore.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal; to let exhaustion become acceptance; to let outrage become memory loss.
> 
> **Tired is not the same as defeated.**
> 
> You counted on outrage lasting 24 hours, on the next scandal burying the last, on everyone moving on. You counted wrong.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal. I refuse to stop being angry simply because there’s too much to be angry about.
> 
> As Angela Davis put it:
> “I am no longer accepting the things I cannot change.  
> I am changing the things I cannot accept.”
> 
> **So we’re saving the receipts.** Every single fucking one. Because shame doesn’t seem to be working. And that’s okay. 
> **Accountability doesn’t require your cooperation.**
> The receipt's getting longer every day. See you next time.
