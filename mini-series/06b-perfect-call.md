## Cold open

**#62** Four hundred million dollars in military aid, already approved by
Congress, for a country fighting Russia. He held it back to squeeze out an
investigation of his opponent. The House impeached him. You said "perfect call,"
because he told you to. And you didn't care.

## The run

**#63** You didn't care that his own lawyer told the *New York Times*, "We're not
meddling in an election, we're meddling in an investigation." That's a
confession with a byline.

**#64** You didn't care that after being impeached for asking one country for
election help, he stood on the White House lawn and asked China, on camera.

**#65** You didn't care that the Senate acquitted him without hearing from Bolton
or Giuliani. Nobody who was in the room was allowed to talk, and you called it
vindication.

**#66** You didn't care that he signed a thirty-five-thousand-dollar hush-money
check from his personal account while sitting in the Oval Office.

## Closer

**#67** And you didn't care that he said a president under criminal
investigation would find it "impossible to govern." Then he spent most of his
presidency under investigation. By his own standard, he was unfit. His words,
not ours.

## Sign-off

> 
> To the people who made all this happen: **We noticed.** The lies. The cruelty. The corruption. The hypocrisy. Every chance to do right you looked directly in the eye and decided to ignore.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal; to let exhaustion become acceptance; to let outrage become memory loss.
> 
> **Tired is not the same as defeated.**
> 
> You counted on outrage lasting 24 hours, on the next scandal burying the last, on everyone moving on. You counted wrong.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal. I refuse to stop being angry simply because there’s too much to be angry about.
> 
> As Angela Davis put it:
> “I am no longer accepting the things I cannot change.  
> I am changing the things I cannot accept.”
> 
> **So we’re saving the receipts.** Every single fucking one. Because shame doesn’t seem to be working. And that’s okay. 
> **Accountability doesn’t require your cooperation.**
> The receipt's getting longer every day. See you next time.
