## Cold open

**#40** Thirty thousand. That's how many false or misleading claims the
*Washington Post* logged in his first term, and the rate went up every year. You
don't have a president who lies. You have a lie with a president attached. And
you didn't care.

## The run

**#41** You didn't care that about one in eight of his tweets was a personal
insult. The leader of the free world, name-calling on his phone like a divorced
uncle at 2 a.m.

**#42** You didn't care that his Secretary of State found out he'd been fired
from a tweet. So did his national security adviser.

**#43** You didn't care that he asked the FBI Director to think about putting
journalists in prison. "Don't Tread on Me" on your license plate.

**#44** You didn't care that he turned "fake news" into a name for real news he
didn't like, while passing around actual fabrications from the *National
Enquirer*, Alex Jones and Russian state media.

**#45** You didn't care when he told four congresswomen to "go back" where they
came from. Three were born here.

**#47** You didn't care about "==shithole==^[🟠 optional bleep] countries." The
party of Christian values, sorting God's children by where he'd take a
vacation.

**#48** You didn't care when he said windmills spew "tremendous fumes" and that
"the world is tiny compared to the universe." You'd have had a Democrat
committed for that.

## Closer

**#46** And you didn't care about "very fine people on both sides," said about a
rally organized by ==white nationalists==^[✏️ caption: "white n@tionalists"]. He
went looking for fine people in a crowd carrying ==Nazi==^[✏️ caption: "N@zi"]
flags, and you found a way to defend it.
