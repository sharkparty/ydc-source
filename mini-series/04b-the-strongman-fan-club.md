## Cold open

**#59** Kim Jong Un. Putin. Duterte. Erdoğan. El-Sisi. He had nothing but praise for
every strongman on the planet and nothing but insults for every ally we have.
You'd call anyone else who did that a traitor. And you didn't care.

## The run

**#54** You didn't care that he told G7 leaders Crimea is Russian and pushed to let
Russia back into the club. He blessed the land grab and asked the victims'
friends to invite the invader to dinner.

**#55** You didn't care that his Treasury lifted sanctions on companies controlled
by a Putin-aligned oligarch. Eleven Republican senators voted against him. You
didn't blink.

**#57** You didn't care that Russia kept creeping into Georgia, the country,
moving the border fences at night a few hundred meters at a time, and his
administration did nothing.

**#58** You didn't care when Belarus rose up against Putin's puppet after a rigged
election and America basically shrugged. You love freedom right up until it
inconveniences Moscow.

**#61** You didn't care that his personal lawyer pressed him and Turkey's president
to free a gold trader charged in the biggest Iran sanctions-evasion case ever
prosecuted here. You'd hang a Democrat for going soft on Iran.

## Closer

**#60** And you didn't care that the CIA concluded the Saudi Crown Prince ordered
Jamal Khashoggi ==murdered==^[✏️ caption: "m*rdered"] and dismembered, and he
defended the Crown Prince and went around Congress to push the arms sales
through. A journalist was cut to pieces, and he treated the man responsible
like a valued customer.
