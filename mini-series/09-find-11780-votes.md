## Cold open

**#98** "I just want to find eleven thousand, seven hundred and eighty votes."
That was Biden's margin in Georgia, plus one. It was recorded, and he's never
denied saying it. He called it a "perfect" call. And you didn't care.

## The run

**#99** You didn't care that he told the Justice Department to "just say the
election was corrupt and leave the rest to me." When they refused, he tried to
install a loyalist to do it. What stopped him was the whole leadership of the
DOJ threatening to quit at once.

**#100** You didn't care that he sent a mob to the Capitol and stopped the
counting of electoral votes for the first time in American history. More than
one hundred forty police officers were injured. You fly the thin blue line flag.

**#101** You didn't care that Mitch McConnell, of all people, said he was
"practically and morally responsible."

**#102** You didn't care that he became the only president in American history
impeached twice.

**#95** You didn't care that three weeks after his first acquittal, he handed
clemency to a batch of mostly corrupt officials and fraudsters, including Rod
Blagojevich, who tried to *sell a Senate seat*.

**#96** You didn't care that he pardoned his campaign chairman and Roger Stone,
the men who could have talked about him and didn't.

**#97** You didn't care that he pardoned Jared's father, who hired a
==prostitute==^[✏️ caption: "pr0stitute"] to seduce his own brother-in-law,
taped it, and mailed the tape to his own sister. That's the family you let into
the White House.

**#106** You didn't care that Georgia charged him and eighteen others under its
racketeering law. RICO. The law they wrote for the mob.

**#185** You didn't care that his Justice Department opened a "grand conspiracy"
investigation into the people who investigated Russian interference. He's not
investigating the crime. He's investigating the investigators.

## Closer

**#184** And you didn't care that once he was back in power, his FBI raided the
Fulton County election office and took the 2020 ballots, the very evidence in
the case against him. He was indicted for trying to get his hands on Georgia's
election records. Then he sent the FBI to take them.
