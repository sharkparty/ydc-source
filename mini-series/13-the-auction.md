## Cold open

**#127** He auctioned off dinner with the President of the United States through
his own memecoin. The top two hundred twenty buyers spent a combined hundred and
forty-eight million dollars. Most of them appeared to be overseas. The top
bidder's SEC fraud case had been put on hold. One company admitted in an SEC
filing it bought in to lobby on trade policy. He stayed twenty-three minutes and
left by helicopter. His press secretary called it "his personal time." And you
didn't care.

## The run

**#128** You didn't care that he took a four-hundred-million-dollar jet from the
government of Qatar, and said it goes to his presidential library when he leaves.

**#129** You didn't care that he approved a hundred and forty-two billion dollars
in Saudi arms while Jared's firm held two billion dollars of the Crown Prince's
money. Jared got paid, the Saudis got F-35s, and you got a lecture about Hunter's
laptop.

**#130** You didn't care that the Saudi-funded LIV Golf tour held tournaments at
his courses while the Saudis poured billions into his family's ventures. The
country fifteen of the 9/11 hijackers came from was paying green fees to the
president, and your "Never Forget" shirt didn't make a sound.

**#157** You didn't care that an Abu Dhabi royal's firm bought half the family
crypto business for about five hundred million dollars, and two weeks later the
UAE got America's most advanced AI chips. First the money, then the chips.

**#160** You didn't care that he sued his own government for ten billion
dollars, then "settled" with himself for a one-point-eight-billion-dollar fund
he controls, and January 6 defendants can collect from it.

**#138** You didn't care that he sells a sixty-dollar Bible out of the White
House. Jesus flipped tables over less.

## Closer

**#180** And you didn't care that Truth Social now sells his posts to Wall Street
traders milliseconds before you see them, for up to a hundred thousand dollars a
month. Fortune's summary of the experts: "insider trading by definition." You're
not his base. You're his exit liquidity.
