## Cold open

**#131** June 2025. He bombed Iran without asking Congress. "No new wars" lasted
five months. And you didn't care.

## The run

**#139** You didn't care that he started blowing up boats in the Caribbean. At
least sixty-four strikes, at least two hundred ten ==dead==^[✏️ caption: "d3ad"],
and he posted the videos like highlight reels. Two of them were fishermen from
Trinidad coming home from work.

**#140** You didn't care that the secret legal memo behind it reportedly shields
the people who carried it out from future prosecution. They lined up the
immunity before they started.

**#151** You didn't care that he sent troops into Caracas, seized a head of
state, and announced at the same press conference that American oil companies
would "go in… and start making money."

**#152** You didn't care that before the raid even happened, he controlled
Venezuelan oil money through an offshore account in Qatar and handed two hundred
fifty million dollars of it to a trading firm whose senior trader gave six
million to his campaign.

**#166** You didn't care when he demanded Israel pardon Netanyahu in his
corruption trial and called Israel's president "disgraceful" for saying no. He
fixes his friends' trials in other countries too.

**#167** You didn't care that in February 2026 he started an open war with Iran
without Congress, and his report cited no authority but his own.

**#178** You didn't care that he announced in writing that every time Iran fired
on a ship, he'd "bomb and destroy ONE BRIDGE OR POWER PLANT," and threatened
their desalination plants. That's drinking water, with an exclamation point.

**#177** You didn't care that Congress passed a war powers resolution for the
first time in fifty years, and the war just kept going.

**#179** You didn't care that a UN fact-finding mission found "reasonable
grounds" to believe the United States committed war crimes. In your name. On
your dime.

*(Beat. Two seconds of silence.)*

## Closer

**#168** And you didn't care that on the first day of that war, three American
missiles hit a girls' elementary school in Minab, during class. About a hundred
and sixty-five people ==killed==^[✏️ caption: "k!lled"], most of them girls
between seven and twelve. The targeting data was years out of date, and nobody
checked. One child, Makan Nasiri, was still missing two months later. You call
yourselves pro-life. Your concern for children has always ended at birth, at a
woman's right to her own body, at the border, at the city limits, or at the
blast radius.
