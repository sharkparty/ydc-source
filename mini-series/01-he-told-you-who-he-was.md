## Cold open

**#5** In 1989 he spent eighty-five thousand dollars ($240,429.19) on full-page ads demanding
that five Black and Latino teenagers be put to death. DNA proved they were
innocent. He never apologized. And you didn't care.

## The run

**#1** You didn't care that the Justice Department sued him for refusing to rent
to Black tenants. That was his debut.

**#2** You didn't care that New York City sued him for harassing rent-controlled
tenants out of their homes. "Champion of the working class." He'd have evicted
you for the square footage.

**#3** You didn't care that "self-made" meant four hundred and thirteen million
dollars from Daddy, moved through tax schemes the *New York Times* found
included outright fraud.

**#4** You didn't care that, according to ProPublica, his company bribed New York
City tax assessors. You pay your property taxes. He paid the assessor.

**#7** You didn't care that when the real banks wouldn't touch him, mob-linked
partners and Russian money did. His own son said it: "Russians make up a pretty
disproportionate cross-section of a lot of our assets."

**#19** You didn't care that his company put its name on a tower in Azerbaijan
with partners tied to Iran's Revolutionary Guard, the branch that buys missile
parts. You'd deport a line cook on a hunch.

**#8** You didn't care that he didn't even write the book that made him famous.
*The Art of the Deal* was ghostwritten, and the ghostwriter has spent years
apologizing for it. Your genius needed someone else to write down how smart he
was.

**#9** You didn't care about Trump Airlines, Trump Steaks, the whole parade of
failures. A man who couldn't sell steak to Americans, and you handed him the
economy.

**#20** You didn't care that years before the pandemic, he was tweeting anti-vaccine
conspiracy theories. He told you who he was about medicine long before he got a
say over yours.

## Closer

**#6** And you didn't care that he bankrupted six companies. Casinos included.
The house always wins. He *was* the house, and he still lost. That's who you
hired to run the economy.

## Sign-off


> Congratulations to everyone who survived another harrowing episode of **“How The Fuck Is This Still Happening?”**
> 
> It’s been a lot. A genuinely fucking unbelievable amount of a lot. If you’re tired, you’re not fucking imagining it.
> 
> To the people who made all this happen: **We noticed.** The lies. The cruelty. The corruption. The hypocrisy. Every chance to do right you looked directly in the eye and decided to ignore.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal; to let exhaustion become acceptance; to let outrage become memory loss.
> 
> **Tired is not the same as defeated.**
> 
> You counted on outrage lasting 24 hours, on the next scandal burying the last, on everyone moving on. You counted wrong.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal. I refuse to stop being angry simply because there’s too much to be angry about.
> 
> As Angela Davis put it:
> “I am no longer accepting the things I cannot change.  
> I am changing the things I cannot accept.”
> 
> **So we’re saving the receipts.** Every single fucking one. Because shame doesn’t seem to be working. And that’s okay. 
> **Accountability doesn’t require your cooperation.**
> The receipt's getting longer every day. See you next time.
