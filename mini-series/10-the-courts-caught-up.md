## Cold open

**#103** According to the indictment, he kept classified documents, including
material on U.S. nuclear programs, in a bathroom and a shower at his resort. You
chanted "lock her up" over an email server. And you didn't care.

## The run

**#104** You didn't care that his company and its CFO were convicted of a
fifteen-year tax fraud. The CFO went to jail. You'd get audited over a missing
W-2.

**#105** You didn't care that a New York court found him, his sons and ten of his
companies liable for fraud across two hundred made-up valuations. The penalty
got tossed on appeal. The finding of fraud did not.

**#107** You didn't care that a special counsel indicted him on four federal
felony counts for conspiring to overturn a presidential election.

**#109** You didn't care that a jury convicted him on thirty-four felony counts.
He's the only president in American history with a criminal conviction. You
made the T-shirt.

**#108** You didn't care that on the day Russia invaded Ukraine, he called Putin
"genius" and "savvy." Then on Fox he called it "the amphibious attack by
Americans," and the host had to tell him whose attack it was.

**#110** You didn't care that every case that reached a verdict went against him.
Every single one. Not one judge or jury ever looked at the evidence and sided
with him.

## Closer

**#111** And you didn't care that winning the election is how he got out of the
federal cases. One was abandoned and one was thrown out on a technicality. The
conduct was never tried. You didn't elect a president. You bought him a
get-out-of-jail card.

## Sign-off


> Congratulations to everyone who survived another harrowing episode of **“How The Fuck Is This Still Happening?”**
> 
> It’s been a lot. A genuinely fucking unbelievable amount of a lot. If you’re tired, you’re not fucking imagining it.
> 
> To the people who made all this happen: **We noticed.** The lies. The cruelty. The corruption. The hypocrisy. Every chance to do right you looked directly in the eye and decided to ignore.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal; to let exhaustion become acceptance; to let outrage become memory loss.
> 
> **Tired is not the same as defeated.**
> 
> You counted on outrage lasting 24 hours, on the next scandal burying the last, on everyone moving on. You counted wrong.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal. I refuse to stop being angry simply because there’s too much to be angry about.
> 
> As Angela Davis put it:
> “I am no longer accepting the things I cannot change.  
> I am changing the things I cannot accept.”
> 
> **So we’re saving the receipts.** Every single fucking one. Because shame doesn’t seem to be working. And that’s okay. 
> **Accountability doesn’t require your cooperation.**
> The receipt's getting longer every day. See you next time.
