## Cold open

**#26** "Russia, if you're listening." He said it on live TV, asking a hostile
foreign intelligence service to find his opponent's emails. That same day,
Russian operatives went after Clinton's office for the first time. You didn't
care.

## The run

**#25** You didn't care that his son, his son-in-law and his campaign chairman
took a meeting after being promised dirt "from Russia and its government." Don
Jr.'s reply, which he published himself: "If it's what you say, I love it."

**#28** You didn't care that his campaign chairman was Paul Manafort, the man
who ran the campaign of Putin's guy in Ukraine and later pleaded guilty to
conspiracy against the United States.

**#27** You didn't care that he was chasing a Trump Tower in Moscow for most of
the campaign while telling you he had no business in Russia.

**#49** You didn't care that he fired the FBI Director who was investigating it,
then tried to fire the special counsel who took over, then told his lawyer to
lie about that.

**#50** You didn't care that Mueller wrote, in so many words, that his report
"does not exonerate him." You read that and heard "total exoneration."

**#51** You didn't care that six of his people were convicted: his campaign
chairman, his deputy chairman, his national security adviser, his personal
lawyer. Six convictions, and you called it a hoax.

**#52** You didn't care that the FBI opened an inquiry into whether the
President of the United States was "wittingly or unwittingly" working for
Russia. The FBI had to ask. You never did.

**#53** You didn't care when he stood next to Putin in Helsinki and took the KGB
man's word over every American intelligence agency, on live TV.

## Closer

**#56** And you didn't care when, after one phone call, he pulled out of northern
Syria and abandoned the Kurds, the people who did the dying against ISIS for us.
More than a hundred ISIS prisoners walked out. Russian troops moved into our
base within weeks. The party of national security let ISIS out of jail and gave
Putin the keys.

## Sign-off


> Congratulations to everyone who survived another harrowing episode of **“How The Fuck Is This Still Happening?”**
> 
> It’s been a lot. A genuinely fucking unbelievable amount of a lot. If you’re tired, you’re not fucking imagining it.
> 
> To the people who made all this happen: **We noticed.** The lies. The cruelty. The corruption. The hypocrisy. Every chance to do right you looked directly in the eye and decided to ignore.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal; to let exhaustion become acceptance; to let outrage become memory loss.
> 
> **Tired is not the same as defeated.**
> 
> You counted on outrage lasting 24 hours, on the next scandal burying the last, on everyone moving on. You counted wrong.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal. I refuse to stop being angry simply because there’s too much to be angry about.
> 
> As Angela Davis put it:
> “I am no longer accepting the things I cannot change.  
> I am changing the things I cannot accept.”
> 
> **So we’re saving the receipts.** Every single fucking one. Because shame doesn’t seem to be working. And that’s okay. 
> **Accountability doesn’t require your cooperation.**
> The receipt's getting longer every day. See you next time.
