**Coverage:** all 185 receipts are in a script, and each appears in exactly
one episode. There are no reserve lists. The counts above are checked against
the scripts: every number in an episode's frontmatter appears in its script as
a bold **#N** tag, and nothing else does. Four themes were too long for one
video, so they're split into A- and B-parts: 04/04B, 06/06B, 08/08B and
13/13B.

The episodes are grouped by theme, not strict date. Episode 7 (the economy)
jumps across both terms, so it puts the year on screen for each line.

## The episode skeleton

1. **Content-warning card** (2 seconds of text, not narrated), for episodes
   that carry one.
2. **Cold open** (first 2 seconds): the hook line. No intro.
3. **The run:** every receipt in the episode, one "You didn't care" line and
   one hard cut each, with the source headline on screen.
4. **Counter overlay:** "Receipt #N of 185" in a fixed corner.
5. **Closer:** the worst line in the episode, saved for last.
6. **Sign-off:** the address switches from *them* to *your audience*.
7. **Comment prompt**, pinned.

## Flag legend

Words in scripts that TikTok moderation may flag are ==highlighted== with an
inline note (shown as a footnote in Obsidian's reading view).

| Marker | Meaning | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔇 **BLEEP** | Bleep the audio. The note gives a neutral on-screen caption. | Sexual violence and crimes against children: rape, sexual abuse, molesting, child sex trafficking, CSAM. Also "pussy" in the quoted tape. |
| ✏️ **caption** | Keep the audio. Soften only the on-screen caption. | Death and violence words: killed, dead, shot, bomb, some slurs or extremist terms. |
| 🟠 **optional** | Your call. Mild profanity TikTok usually allows. | shit, shithole |

**Why bleeps instead of substitutes like "grape":** substitutes read as a joke,
and these are real survivors, including children. A bleep with a neutral caption
("SA," "abuse") keeps the respect and still gets past the filters.

**Visuals:** across the series, no footage or images of shootings, the Capitol
beatings, Minab, or abuse victims. Headlines, documents, names on black cards.

## Accuracy rules for recording

These come straight from the repo's own conventions. They're what keep the
series from being debunked.

- **Liable ≠ convicted.** Carroll and the New York fraud case are civil
  findings. The 34 felonies are the conviction.
- **Allegations stay allegations.** #13 and #158 say "alleged," "never tested,"
  "may not be true." Say those words on camera.
- **Timing isn't a charge.** #123 (tariff post), #134 (Maxwell), #142 (Binance)
  and #169 (drones) are sequences. Say them in order and let viewers draw the
  line. Don't say "insider trading," "rewarded" or "bribe" as facts.
- **Mueller did not establish a conspiracy.** Don't say "collusion was proven."
- **No debunked Epstein mention counts.** The repo corrects them.
