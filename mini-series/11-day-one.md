## Cold open

**#113** Hours after taking the oath, he pardoned about fifteen hundred January 6
rioters, including the men who beat Officer Michael Fanone until he had a heart
attack. You were "Back the Blue" right up until the blue was bleeding on the
Capitol steps. And you didn't care.

## The run

**#114** You didn't care that he freed the Oath Keepers and Proud Boys leaders
convicted of seditious conspiracy. Eighteen years for one, twenty-two for the
other. Gone.

**#115** You didn't care when his administration went back to erase the
seditious conspiracy convictions entirely. Pardoning them wasn't enough.

**#117** You didn't care that congressional Republicans had promised you the
violent ones would be left out. They lied, and you believed them.

**#112** You didn't care that three days before the oath, he launched a memecoin
with his face on it, with eighty percent held by his own trust. According to two
senators, investors lost three point eight billion dollars and he made six
hundred thirty-six million. He ran a pump-and-dump on his own voters.

**#118** You didn't care that on day one he froze all foreign aid, then shut
USAID down entirely. Researchers project millions of deaths from the lost HIV,
malaria and food programs. That's a projection, not a count, but the medicine
stopped either way. You called it "waste."

**#119** You didn't care that he shut down the agency that gets your money back
when a bank screws you. His guy bragged that its funding "spigot" was being
"turned off."

## Closer

**#116** And you didn't care when Officer Fanone said his family was "less safe
today because of Donald Trump and his supporters." He was talking about you.

## Sign-off


> Congratulations to everyone who survived another harrowing episode of **“How The Fuck Is This Still Happening?”**
> 
> It’s been a lot. A genuinely fucking unbelievable amount of a lot. If you’re tired, you’re not fucking imagining it.
> 
> To the people who made all this happen: **We noticed.** The lies. The cruelty. The corruption. The hypocrisy. Every chance to do right you looked directly in the eye and decided to ignore.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal; to let exhaustion become acceptance; to let outrage become memory loss.
> 
> **Tired is not the same as defeated.**
> 
> You counted on outrage lasting 24 hours, on the next scandal burying the last, on everyone moving on. You counted wrong.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal. I refuse to stop being angry simply because there’s too much to be angry about.
> 
> As Angela Davis put it:
> “I am no longer accepting the things I cannot change.  
> I am changing the things I cannot accept.”
> 
> **So we’re saving the receipts.** Every single fucking one. Because shame doesn’t seem to be working. And that’s okay. 
> **Accountability doesn’t require your cooperation.**
> The receipt's getting longer every day. See you next time.
