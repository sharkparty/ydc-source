## Cold open

**#145** The House voted four hundred twenty-seven to one to force the Epstein
files out, after months of him fighting it. Even his own party turned on him
over this one. You didn't.

## The run

**#36** You didn't care that back in his first term, his Labor Secretary was the
prosecutor who gave Epstein his 2007 sweetheart deal: thirteen months, with work
release sixteen hours a day.

**#133** You didn't care that he denied being told his name was in the files,
when his own Attorney General had told him two months earlier.

**#159** You didn't care that the man put in charge of releasing the files was
his own former criminal defense lawyer. The accused's lawyer decided what the
public got to see.

**#158** You didn't care that his Justice Department held back FBI interviews
with a woman who said Trump ==sexually abused==^[🔇 BLEEP · caption: "SA'd"] her
when she was a minor, even though the law said release them. The accusation has
never been tested. But they hid it, got caught, and then said they were
"examining whether" they'd withheld it wrongly.

**#135** You didn't care that they then rewrote the prison rules so the Attorney
General personally decides where any federal prisoner goes, without having to
explain it to anyone.

## Closer

**#134** And you didn't care that his former defense lawyer sat down with
Ghislaine Maxwell for nine hours. She said she'd never seen Trump do anything
wrong. One week later she was moved to a minimum-security camp that the
Bureau of Prisons' own rules bar ==child sex traffickers==^[🔇 BLEEP "sex" ·
caption: "child tr*ffickers"] from, and the reason for the exception is blacked
out. You spent years screaming "save the children." Then a convicted trafficker
got a cushier cell for saying the right thing about your guy, and you went
quiet.
