## Cold open

**#86** He declared a national emergency over a caravan of women and children
walking toward the border. For a pandemic, he waited until March thirteenth.
And you didn't care.

## The run

**#85** You didn't care that he told the states to buy their own masks, then had
the federal government bid against them. A pandemic run like an eBay auction.

**#87** You didn't care that Republican senators were briefed in January and
adjusted their stock portfolios before the emergency declaration. They took care
of their money while you went to church.

**#88** You didn't care that he pushed chloroquine from the podium, and a man in
Arizona ==died==^[✏️ caption: "d!ed"] after taking chloroquine phosphate.

**#89** You didn't care that he crossed out "coronavirus" in his speech and wrote
"Chinese virus" by hand, and the cameras caught it. George Takei called it "a
signal to the haters." The haters got the message.

**#90** You didn't care that in the middle of it all, he tried to cut food
stamps for seven hundred thousand people, until a judge stopped him.

**#91** You didn't care that his Labor Department asked states to hold off on
publishing unemployment numbers while claims exploded. When the numbers looked
bad, hide the numbers.

**#92** You didn't care that he threatened to veto oversight of the bailout. Your
tax money went out the door, and he didn't want anyone checking where it
went.

**#93** You didn't care that he tried to pay a German company to make a vaccine
"only for the United States." A pandemic, handled like a real estate deal.

## Closer

**#94** And you didn't care when he looked at a terrified country and refused to
take any responsibility at all. Harry Truman had a sign on his desk that said
"The buck stops here." Trump had a Sharpie and a grudge.

## Sign-off


> Congratulations to everyone who survived another harrowing episode of **“How The Fuck Is This Still Happening?”**
> 
> It’s been a lot. A genuinely fucking unbelievable amount of a lot. If you’re tired, you’re not fucking imagining it.
> 
> To the people who made all this happen: **We noticed.** The lies. The cruelty. The corruption. The hypocrisy. Every chance to do right you looked directly in the eye and decided to ignore.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal; to let exhaustion become acceptance; to let outrage become memory loss.
> 
> **Tired is not the same as defeated.**
> 
> You counted on outrage lasting 24 hours, on the next scandal burying the last, on everyone moving on. You counted wrong.
> 
> I refuse to pretend this is normal. I refuse to stop being angry simply because there’s too much to be angry about.
> 
> As Angela Davis put it:
> “I am no longer accepting the things I cannot change.  
> I am changing the things I cannot accept.”
> 
> **So we’re saving the receipts.** Every single fucking one. Because shame doesn’t seem to be working. And that’s okay. 
> **Accountability doesn’t require your cooperation.**
> The receipt's getting longer every day. See you next time.
