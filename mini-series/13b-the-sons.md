## Cold open

**#169** A week into his war with Iran, a drone company announced his sons as
investors. It was going after a one-point-one-billion-dollar Pentagon drone
program. Eric posted, "Drones are the wave of the future." Dad starts the war,
the boys sell the drones. And you didn't care.

## The run

**#170** You didn't care that Eric also invested in an Israeli drone maker that
landed a U.S. military contract, and Don Jr. sits on the advisory board of
another drone company that got fifteen million dollars in military-linked
orders.

**#171** You didn't care that after his administration signed a billion-dollar
tungsten mining deal with Kazakhstan, his sons turned up as shareholders in the
mining company.

**#172** You didn't care that his administration stopped enforcing the law
against bribing foreign officials. You spent your whole life afraid of becoming
Russia or China. Now we're copying their approach to bribery.

**#173** You didn't care that his businesses reported at least a hundred and
twenty-five million dollars from foreign sources in a single year. You'd get
fired for taking a gift card from a vendor.

**#174** You didn't care that the family's crypto ventures made roughly one point
four billion dollars in the first year of the second term. You were promised
the economy would be fixed. It was fixed, for them.

**#181** You didn't care that he promoted more than twenty companies on Truth
Social within days of buying their stock. Pumping his own stocks, from the
presidency.

**#183** You didn't care that a White House aide handled government secrets for a
year without a security clearance, then got part of forty-five thousand dollars
in cash gifts from the president. "Lock her up" was only ever about the "her."

## Closer

**#176** And you didn't care that a Putin-linked Russian oligarch paid for much of
the celebration around Don Jr.'s wedding, a private island and fireworks
included. The family called it "an extraordinarily generous wedding gift from a
friend." Trump said it was repaid and never showed proof. A Russian oligarch
paid for the president's son's wedding, and you couldn't even be bothered to be
surprised.
