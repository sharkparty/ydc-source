## Phase 1 — Real estate, business, and the 2016 campaign

The New York years established the public profile and, with it, a pattern of
civil litigation and regulatory trouble that never stopped.

| Year | | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1973 | [Personal] | **DOJ Fair Housing lawsuit.** The Justice Department sued Trump and his father for refusing to rent apartments to Black tenants. Settled in 1975 with no admission of guilt. ([Legal Record](legal-record.md)) |
| 1985 | [Personal] | **Central Park South harassment.** New York City sued Trump over aggressive tactics used to drive rent-controlled tenants out of a building he intended to demolish. ([Legal Record](legal-record.md)) |
| 1989 | [Personal] | **The Central Park Five.** Trump spent $85,000 on full-page ads calling for the return of the death penalty for five Black and Latino teenagers, wrongfully convicted. He has refused to apologize since their exoneration by DNA evidence. ([Character and Conduct](character-and-conduct.md#4-racism)) |
| 1990 | [Personal] | Six corporate bankruptcies begin — Atlantic City casinos, the Plaza, the Castle. ([Business Record](business-and-fraud.md#2-the-failures)) |
| 1996 | [Personal] | **E. Jean Carroll.** The sexual abuse for which two civil juries would later find him liable, awarding over $88 million. ([Sexual Misconduct](sexual-misconduct.md)) |
| 2005 | [Personal] | The *Access Hollywood* recording is made. ([Character and Conduct](character-and-conduct.md#3-grab-them-by-the-pussy)) |
| 2013 | [Personal] | **Trump University fraud.** Class actions and a New York Attorney General probe over deceptive marketing at the unaccredited seminar program. Settled for $25 million just before the inauguration. ([Legal Record](legal-record.md)) |
| 2016 (Jun) | [International] | **Trump Tower meeting.** Donald Trump Jr., Jared Kushner, and Paul Manafort met a Kremlin-connected lawyer after being promised damaging material on Hillary Clinton "from Russia and its government." ([Russia and Foreign Policy](russia-and-foreign-policy.md)) |
| 2016 (Jul) | [International] | **"Russia, if you're listening."** At a televised press conference, Trump publicly invited Russia to find his opponent's missing emails. ([Russia and Foreign Policy](russia-and-foreign-policy.md)) |
| 2016 (Oct) | [Personal] | The *Access Hollywood* tape is published. ([Character and Conduct](character-and-conduct.md#3-grab-them-by-the-pussy)) |
| 2016–17 | [Personal] | The Trump Tower Moscow deal is pursued during the campaign and publicly denied. ([Business Record](business-and-fraud.md#9-trump-tower-moscow)) |

The controversies shift from private business disputes to constitutional ones.

| Year | | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2017 (Jan) | [International] | **The travel ban.** An executive order barring travel from several Muslim-majority countries, producing mass airport detentions before the courts struck it down. |

| 2019 (Oct) | [International] | **Abandoning the Kurds.** After a call with Erdoğan, Trump withdrew U.S. troops from northern Syria, clearing the way for a Turkish offensive against America's ground allies against ISIS. | 2020 (Feb) | [National] | **The clemency batch** — seven pardons and four commutations, mostly public corruption and financial fraud. ([Pardons](pardons-and-clemency.md)) |
| 2020 (Jan–Dec) | [National] | **COVID-19.** ([COVID-19 Response](covid-19-response.md)) |
| 2020 (Jul) | [Personal] | **Mary L. Trump's lawsuit** alleging the family committed financial fraud to siphon money from her inheritance. ([Legal Record](legal-record.md)) |
| 2020 (Dec) | [National] | **Subversion of the Justice Department.** Pressure on DOJ leadership to declare the election corrupt; the attempt to install Jeffrey Clark as acting Attorney General. ([Election Subversion](election-subversion-and-january-6.md#2-pressuring-the-justice-department)) |
| 2020 (Dec) | [International] | **Pardoning the Blackwater contractors** convicted of the 2007 Nisour Square massacre, an unprovoked attack that killed 14 unarmed Iraqi civilians. ([Pardons](pardons-and-clemency.md)) |
| 2020 (Dec) | [Personal] | **Pardons for Manafort, Stone, and Charles Kushner.** ([Pardons](pardons-and-clemency.md#december-2020-the-associates)) |

## On sourcing

Entries above that carry a link are sourced in the file they point to. Entries
without one came from a compilation that provided no citations for them — the
1973 housing suit, the 1985 harassment suit, the travel ban, the Oval Office
intelligence disclosure, family separation, the border wall diversion, and the
Blackwater pardons among them.

These are all well-documented events and none is seriously disputed. They are
still uncited here, and this repository's standard is that an uncited claim is a
liability. Adding the primary sources for them is the most useful contribution
anyone could make to this page.
