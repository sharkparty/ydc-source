# You Didn't Care

This is an itemized receipt, addressed to one reader: the person who
looked at all of this and voted for him anyway. Some of you did it three times.

Every line below links to the file in this repository where the fact is sourced.
The contempt is ours. The facts are not, and they hold up without it. Nothing
here says "convicted" where the record says "liable," and nothing says
"proven" where the record says "alleged." We held the line on that for our own
sake, not yours.

It runs in chronological order, the way you lived through it, one day at a time,
never once looking up.
