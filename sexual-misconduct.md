## The accusers

At least 17 women publicly accused Trump of sexual assault or misconduct before
the 2016 election, with accounts ranging from forcible kissing to groping to
assault, many of them corroborated by contemporaneous accounts told to friends
and family.

- [Quartz, "17 women have accused Donald Trump of sexual assault or misconduct"](https://qz.com/1130324/17-women-have-accused-donald-trump-of-sexual-assault-or-misconduct-its-time-to-revisit-those-stories/)
- [Snopes, on the 2016 rape lawsuit](https://www.snopes.com/news/2016/06/23/donald-trump-rape-lawsuit/)
- [Full catalogue of allegations, with sources and dates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_sexual_misconduct_allegations)

**Context:** The count has grown since this list was compiled. More than two
dozen women have now made public accusations. In May 2023 a federal jury in New
York found Trump liable for sexually abusing and defaming E. Jean Carroll and
awarded $5 million; a second jury awarded $83.3 million in January 2024. The
presiding judge later wrote that the jury's finding was, in ordinary usage,
a finding of rape.

## His own account of his method

The *Access Hollywood* tape is not an allegation. It is a description, in his
own voice, of doing the thing he is accused of doing — kissing and grabbing
women without waiting for consent, on the theory that celebrity makes it
permissible. See [Character and Public Conduct](character-and-conduct.md#3-grab-them-by-the-pussy).

The tape and the accusations corroborate one another, which is the basis for
listing them together.

## The pageant dressing rooms

Multiple contestants — including minors at Miss Teen USA — have described Trump
walking into dressing rooms while they were undressed. Trump described doing
this himself, on the Howard Stern show, as an owner's privilege.
([Allegations of pageant dressing room visits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_sexual_misconduct_allegations#Allegations_of_pageant_dressing_room_visits))

## The 1994 allegation and the withdrawn lawsuit

A woman filed suit alleging that Trump raped her in 1994, when she was 13,
at a series of parties hosted by Jeffrey Epstein.

> "The lawyer who organized the event, Lisa Bloom, said Trump's accuser had
> received threats and was too frightened to show up. In the most recent suit,
> Trump's accuser asserted that while she was exploring a modeling career in
> 1994, she attended a series of parties at the Manhattan home of prominent
> investor Jeffrey Epstein. She alleges that during those parties the real
> estate mogul tied her to a bed and raped her. She also claimed Epstein raped
> her during that series of gatherings."
>
> — [Politico](https://www.politico.com/story/2016/11/donald-trump-rape-lawsuit-dropped-230770)

**See also** [The Epstein Files](epstein-files.md) for the released documents,
the allegations the Justice Department withheld, and the Maxwell transfer.

**Context:** The suit was withdrawn days before the 2016 election. The plaintiff, who filed anonymously, cancelled a
scheduled press conference and dropped the case, with her attorney citing
threats. The allegations were never tested in court and no finding was ever
made. They remain allegations. They are included here because they were filed,
reported, and withdrawn under stated duress — not because a withdrawn complaint
is evidence of anything on its own.
