## 1. Presidential profiteering

Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington (CREW) documented more than
900 distinct conflicts of interest in Trump's second year in office alone —
interactions between the federal government, people seeking to influence it, and
the Trump Organization.

> "President Trump has tried to use the presidency to enrich himself by
> promoting his businesses as extensions of his administration. That includes
> conducting government business at them, praising them in his official
> capacity, and even going so far as to offer exclusive perks to members of his
> clubs including access to government leaders, influence on government
> business, and in some cases, appointments to government positions."
>
> — CREW, *Presidential Profiteering: Trump's Conflicts Got Worse in Year Two*
> ([source](https://www.citizensforethics.org/reports-investigations/crew-reports/presidential-profiteering-trumps-conflicts-got-worse-in-year-two/))

CREW later estimated that Trump businesses took in roughly $13.6 million from
foreign governments during his presidency.
([source](https://www.citizensforethics.org/reports-investigations/crew-reports/trump-likely-benefited-from-13-6-million-in-payments-from-foreign-governments-during-his-presidency/))

### The golf trips

Trump spent a large share of his weekends at properties he personally profits
from, with the federal government paying for the travel, security, and lodging —
some of it paid directly to his own clubs.

The Government Accountability Office found that Trump's **first four** trips to
Mar-a-Lago, between February 3 and March 5, 2017, cost taxpayers **$13.6
million**: roughly $10.6 million in aircraft and vessel operating costs and $3
million in personnel costs, of which about $60,000 went directly to his Florida
club. The estimate excludes classified costs.
([GAO-19-178](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-19-178),
[CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2019/02/05/politics/mar-a-lago-cost-government-accountability-office-report))

For a sense of scale: see the note in
[COVID-19 Response](covid-19-response.md) comparing the pandemic-preparedness
budget he cut against what his golf travel cost.

## 2. Security clearances for family

Trump personally intervened to obtain top-secret clearances for Jared Kushner
and Ivanka Trump after career security officials and White House lawyers
recommended against them — then denied having done so.

- [Ivanka Trump's clearance (CNN)](https://www.cnn.com/2019/03/05/politics/ivanka-trump-security-clearance-pressure/index.html)
- [Jared Kushner's clearance (The New York Times)](https://www.nytimes.com/2019/02/28/us/politics/jared-kushner-security-clearance.html)

Both continued to hold outside business interests while serving in the White
House.

**Correction to claims in circulation:** The clearances were not granted by
executive order. Trump directed his chief of staff to grant them over the
objections of the intelligence and legal staff who had reviewed the files — an
exercise of informal authority rather than formal, which left no corresponding
paper trail.

## 6. The $TRUMP memecoin

**The structure.** $TRUMP launched January 17, 2025. **80% of the supply was
locked to CIC Digital, owned by the Donald J. Trump Revocable Trust** — meaning
the president's trust held four fifths of an asset whose price depended on the
president's fame.

**The congressional finding.** House Oversight Committee Democrats' report of
January 20, 2026: the coin "has exhibited some of the hallmark signs of fraud …
President Trump, and favored allies, would have been uniquely positioned to
profit."
([report, PDF](https://oversightdemocrats.house.gov/imo/media/doc/12026cryptoreport.pdf))

**The numbers.** Senators Warren and Blumenthal, writing to the SEC on August 4,
2026: "nearly a million investors lost over $3.8 billion … while Trump himself
made $636 million." They asked the SEC to investigate a possible "rug pull."
([Senate Banking Committee](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/following-investor-losses-warren-and-blumenthal-urge-sec-to-investigate-president-trumps-memecoin))

**Caveat:** no court or agency has found this to be a scam. The SEC — chaired by
Trump's appointee Paul Atkins — has taken no public action. "Signs of fraud" in a
minority committee report is an allegation by political opponents rather than an
adjudication. The ownership structure, the launch, and the investor losses are
not in dispute.

## 7. The tariff-pause trade

**The record, in two timestamps.**

**April 9, 2025, 9:37 a.m.** — Trump posts on Truth Social:

> "THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!! DJT"

**April 9, 2025, ~1:18 p.m.** — The administration announces the tariff pause.
The S&P 500 surges 9.5%, one of its best single days in history.

**The response.** Letters from Senators Warren, Schumer, Wyden, Schiff, Gallego,
and Kelly, among others, to SEC Chair Atkins and to the White House and Office
of Government Ethics on April 11, 2025, demanding investigation into "insider
trading, market manipulation, or other securities laws violations," plus a
parallel Ways and Means letter to Trump, Bessent, Lutnick, Greer, and Atkins.
([Sen. Schiff](https://www.schiff.senate.gov/news/press-releases/news-sen-schiff-joins-sens-warren-schumer-to-call-on-sec-to-launch-investigation-into-possible-trump-tariff-market-manipulation-insider-trading/))

**No public SEC enforcement action has followed.** The post is public, the
timing is public, the market move is public, and nothing happened.

## 8. Merchandise

**The structure.** Licensing runs through **CIC Ventures LLC and CIC Digital
LLC, wholly owned by the Donald J. Trump Revocable Trust.** The catalogue
includes a $60 Bible, $399–499 sneakers, a $100,000 "Victory Tourbillon" watch,
guitars, and the $499 T1 phone from Trump Mobile with a $47.45/month plan.

Trump's financial disclosures show millions in merchandise income — $4.7 million
from watches alone.
([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/trump-master-merchandise-face-fresh-conflicts-interests-experts/story?id=115912341))

**The honest legal position:** licensing income is not clearly illegal, and
experts including Lawrence Lessig, CREW, and Public Citizen describe it as
unprecedented rather than unlawful. The live question is emoluments and
conflicts of interest, and it has not been litigated to a conclusion. The
objection is not that a statute was broken; it is that a sitting president is
selling branded goods to the public from a trust he owns, and no statute
anticipated needing to forbid it.

## 11. The president's sons and the war they profit from

**The timing is the allegation.**

**Powerus Corporation**, a West Palm Beach drone maker, announced Eric Trump and
Donald Trump Jr. as "notable investors" in a reverse merger with **Aureus
Greenway Holdings — a Nasdaq-listed golf course company.** The announcement came
**March 9, 2026, roughly a week after the Iran war began**, targeting the
Pentagon's $1.1 billion "Drone Dominance" program. The Air Force now buys
Powerus systems.
([AP](https://apnews.com/article/drones-eric-donald-trump-powerus-iran-defense-089bff3892f921a10ef4ec785308e716))

Days after the war began, Eric Trump posted: "Drones are the wave of the future
— Proud of this company."

**Also on the books:**

- Eric Trump is an investor in **Xtend**, an Israeli drone maker that announced
  a "multi-million-dollar U.S. Department of War contract" in November 2025.
- Donald Trump Jr. sits on the advisory board of **Unusual Machines**, which
  subsequently announced $15.2 million in military-linked orders, including a
  direct Army purchase.
  ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/zacheverson/2025/10/29/donald-trump-jr-ultimate-machines-defense-contracts-drones/))

**Caveat:** no court has found a conflict-of-interest violation. It is an
apparent conflict, not an adjudicated one. It is also the president's sons taking
positions in defense contractors immediately after the president started a war
that those contractors sell into, which is the kind of thing that does not
require a court to be recognizable.

## 12. The Lutnick family and the tariff refunds

**What the claim should actually be.** This is often described as "raking in
millions on tariff loan insurance." That is not what happened, and the accurate
version is better.

After Trump imposed the "Liberation Day" tariffs, **Cantor Fitzgerald —
controlled by Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick's sons Brandon and Kyle — spent
millions buying up rights to potential tariff refunds.** That is a bet that the
courts would strike down the tariffs their father's administration was
championing.

**The bet paid.** The Supreme Court struck the tariffs down 6–3 in February
2026, leaving up to roughly **$175 billion in refunds** and a market in refund
claims worth about $100 billion.

**Primary documents:** Representative Raskin's February 27, 2026 letter to
Howard and Brandon Lutnick demanding records
([House Judiciary Democrats press release](http://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/ranking-member-raskin-demands-records-from-secretary-lutnick-son-over-appearance-of-tariff-profiteering),
[the letter itself, PDF](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/democrats-judiciary.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/2026-02-27-raskin-to-lutnick-commerce-lutnick-cantorfitzgerald-re-tariffs.pdf?ref=gst.artofliberty.org));
the SEC filings themselves, including the Cantor Fitzgerald Income Trust 10-K
appointing Brandon to the board on March 5, 2025; and a joint committee letter
of July 6, 2026.

Separately documented: Cantor's best year on record, $2.5 billion in revenue,
November 2025.

**Why this one is strong:** it does not depend on inferring a motive. The
Commerce Secretary's sons' firm took a large financial position that only pays
out if the administration's signature economic policy is illegal. Either they
knew something, or they believed their father's policy would not survive court
review. Both readings are damaging.

## 13. The $1.8 billion slush fund

The mechanism, in order:

1. Trump, his eldest sons, and the Trump Organization **sued the IRS and the
   Treasury Department for $10 billion** over the leak of his tax returns during
   his first term.
2. He controls the IRS and the Treasury.
3. Federal judges questioned whether the two sides were genuinely adverse —
   the defendant agencies answered to the plaintiff — and pressed both parties to
   settle.
4. The settlement created a **$1.8 billion fund**, drawn from the Treasury's
   Judgment Fund, to pay people who believe the government wrongly targeted
   them, **including January 6 defendants.**
5. Awards are approved by a five-member commission Trump controls, with no
   oversight, no disclosure requirement, and no guardrails.

Critics called it "one of the single most corrupt acts in American history."

([Rep. Fletcher](https://fletcher.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=7367),
[Sen. Reed](https://www.reed.senate.gov/news/releases/reed-probes-trumps-corrupt-self-dealing-1776-billion-slush-fund),
[Senate Finance Committee Democrats, on the fund and interference with tax audits](https://www.finance.senate.gov/ranking-members-news/wyden-finance-committee-democrats-call-for-bipartisan-investigation-of-trump-slush-fund-and-interference-with-tax-audits),
[Rep. Tonko](https://tonko.house.gov/news/email/show.aspx?ID=JJGLGLLTMPFHP7FCXQD6D5DPYI))

A president suing agencies he controls, and settling with himself out of the
public treasury, is not a conflict of interest. It is the absence of two parties.

## 14. Truth Social as a trading desk

### The stock promotion

A CNN investigation found Trump used his Truth Social account to **promote more
than 20 companies within days of buying stock in those same companies.**
([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/17/politics/trump-corruption-truth-social?cid=ios_app#1),
[Senate Democrats](https://www.democrats.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/as-americans-suffer-from-higher-costs-trump-continues-to-enrich-himself-and-his-family-turning-public-office-into-private-profit))

### Truth API — selling early access to the president

In August 2026, Trump Media launched **Truth API**, which delivers Trump's Truth
Social posts to paying subscribers **milliseconds before the public sees them.**

- **$60,000 to $100,000 per month.** Trump Media has pitched packages running to
  **$1.2 million** a year.
- **More than ten firms had signed on within weeks**, most of them
  high-frequency trading shops — the buyers for whom milliseconds are the entire
  product.
- Trump Media has been in talks to extend access to news organizations and AI
  companies.

**Why the millisecond matters.** Trump routinely announces policy on Truth Social
— tariffs, personnel, strikes — *sometimes before his own administration knows.*
A trading firm receiving those posts first can position itself before the market
moves. That is the product's stated value proposition, marketed openly.

The company's own sales material establishes the point: the product functions
only if the president's posts move markets and the subscriber receives them
first.

### The response

Senators **Warren and Schiff** asked the SEC to determine whether the
arrangement violates federal securities law, calling it a **"shocking abuse of
the office of the President."** Trump has been sued over it.

Fortune's summary of the expert consensus: **"This is insider trading by
definition."**

([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/11/business/wall-street-trump-truth-social),
[NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/01/nx-s1-5912219/trump-truth-social-access-insider-trading),
[Bloomberg, on the $1.2M pitch](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/trump-media-pitches-1-2-million-truth-api-to-wall-street-traders),
[Fortune, on the subscriber count](https://fortune.com/2026/08/12/trump-media-truth-api-insider-trading/),
[Fortune, "This is insider trading by definition"](https://fortune.com/2026/08/04/insider-trading-trump-truth-social-early-access/),
[PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/paid-early-access-to-trumps-posts-raises-insider-trading-questions),
[Fox News, on the lawsuit](https://www.foxnews.com/media/trumps-100k-wall-street-truth-access-draws-lawsuit-expert-warns-raises-stakes-ethics),
[IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com/trumps-truth-social-posts-have-price-tag-wall-street-firms-are-already-paying-early-access-3806291),
[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/media-advertising/articles/nearly-dozen-firms-already-paying-182609301.html))

### The progression

Sections 7, 14, and 15 describe a single trajectory:

1. **April 2025** — "THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!!" posted hours before the
   tariff pause. On the most charitable reading, an impulsive post.
2. **Ongoing** — companies promoted days after their stock was purchased; 15
   Nvidia trades; Cabinet officials selling ahead of tariff announcements.
3. **August 2026** — the informational advantage packaged as a subscription
   product with a published price and a sales operation.

The first step is consistent with carelessness. The third is a business model,
and a $1.2 million-a-year API is not built around an incidental advantage.

## 15. Trading ahead of announcements

Trump and multiple Cabinet members traded stocks on knowledge of forthcoming
government actions. Trump made **15 separate trades in Nvidia stock** — the
largest on February 10, when $1 million to $5 million in Nvidia shares were
bought on his behalf. Multiple Cabinet members sold ahead of one of the tariff
announcements.
