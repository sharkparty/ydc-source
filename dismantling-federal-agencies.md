# Dismantling Federal Agencies

Three cases where the executive branch shut down functions Congress created and
funded — by starving them, not by repealing them.

## 1. USAID — dismantled

**Documented, and effectively complete.**

**The mechanism.** Executive Order of January 20, 2025, "Reevaluating and
Realigning United States Foreign Aid," imposed a 90-day pause on all foreign
assistance. Secretary of State Marco Rubio enforced it with a stop-work
memorandum on January 24. On March 10, 2025, Rubio announced that 83% of USAID
programs had been cancelled. **USAID formally ceased operations on July 1,
2025.**
([Foley Hoag legal analysis of the shutdown](https://foleyhoag.com/news-and-insights/publications/alerts-and-updates/2025/december/us-policy-shift-on-foreign-aid-key-legal-issues-for-usaid-contractors-ngos-and-life-sciences/))

**The constitutional problem**, stated by Judge Amir Ali of the U.S. District
Court for the District of Columbia:

> "The constitutional power over whether to spend foreign aid is not the
> President's own — and it is Congress's own."

([AP](https://apnews.com/article/usaid-trump-foreign-aid-rubio-judge-ali-60ef55de60a36c61eb563b5982298385))

This is the Impoundment Control Act question from the first impeachment, at a
scale roughly a thousand times larger. In 2019 the offense was withholding $400
million from one country for a few weeks. Here it was the entire foreign
assistance account, permanently.

**What the courts did.** The Supreme Court initially rejected the
administration's appeal. On September 26, 2025, it stayed a lower-court order
requiring payment of approximately $5 billion in already-appropriated funds.

**On the death toll.** Projections of millions of deaths from interrupted HIV,
malaria, tuberculosis, and food programs come from epidemiological modeling.
The modeling literature is substantial and the underlying programs and their
interruption are documented fact, but a model produces a projection rather than a
count, and should be cited as such. The documented record stands independently:
the programs existed, Congress funded them, they stopped, and the populations
dependent on them lost access.

## 2. The CFPB — mostly dismantled, legally blocked from abolition

**The mechanism.** Acting Director Russell Vought's emails of February 8–10,
2025 ordered all staff to "cease all supervision and examination activity" and
stop work entirely. On defunding the agency — which draws its budget from the
Federal Reserve rather than from appropriations — Vought wrote:

> "This spigot, long contributing to CFPB's unaccountability, is now being
> turned off."

([AP](https://apnews.com/article/trump-consumer-protection-cease-1b93c60a773b6b5ee629e769ae6850e9))

**What stopped it.** *NTEU v. Vought.* On December 30, 2025, Judge Amy Berman
Jackson ruled that the White House cannot let the CFPB's Federal Reserve funding
lapse.
([AP](https://apnews.com/article/cfpb-russell-vought-nteu-federal-reserve-banks-965ff497e53aee8fedd37f0cd07c42e3))

**Where it stands.** The agency has been largely inoperable but is not legally
abolished — abolishing it requires an act of Congress, which was never sought.
The distinction matters: the strategy was not to persuade Congress to close the
agency but to make it stop functioning while leaving the statute on the books.

The same pattern recurs across the cases in this file. Repeal is difficult and
subject to review; defunding is neither.

## 3. Climate science — defunded

The primary sources for this section are the administration's own budget
documents.

**"Ending the Green New Scam"** — the White House's own FY2026 fact sheet —
cancels more than $15 billion in Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act
clean-energy funds, cuts $2.5 billion from the Department of Energy's Office of
Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy, ends U.S. contributions to the Global
Environment Facility, and terminates NOAA climate research grants.
([White House, PDF](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Ending-the-Green-New-Scam-Fact-Sheet.pdf))

Also in the budget documents:

- U.S. Global Change Research Program funding ended.
- NOAA's budget request sought to zero out its climate laboratories — including
  the Mauna Loa observatory, which has maintained the continuous atmospheric CO₂
  record since 1958. That record is the single longest direct measurement series
  in climate science; it cannot be reconstructed once broken.
- The National Science Foundation proposal: a 97% cut to USGCRP.

**"Jeopardizing the planet" is a judgment.** The defunding is not — it is
itemized, in the administration's own published budget, under a title the
administration chose.

## 4. Disaster relief, withheld from the wrong states

Trump **overruled his own agencies** to deny disaster aid to four
Democratic-led states — New Jersey, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, and New York —
after administration officials had already verified that each request met the
federal standards for approval.

The emails between state and federal officials, obtained by Politico, are the
record.
([Senate Democrats, summarizing the reporting](https://www.democrats.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/as-americans-suffer-from-higher-costs-trump-continues-to-enrich-himself-and-his-family-turning-public-office-into-private-profit))

This belongs in this file rather than under corruption because it is the same
constitutional question as USAID, one step further along. There, appropriated
money went unspent. Here, appropriated money was spent selectively, on a
partisan test, over the objection of the civil servants who had applied the
statutory criteria and found them met.

Disaster relief is not discretionary largesse. It is a program Congress
authorized with eligibility rules, and those rules do not include which party
won the state.
